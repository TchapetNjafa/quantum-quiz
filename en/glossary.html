Course vocabulary

Glossary

30 terms in alphabetical order. Tap a term to read its definition.

Bell inequalitiesch. 4

Inequalities satisfied by any local hidden-variable theory. Quantum mechanics violates them, and experiments confirm the violation (Nobel Prize 2022: Aspect, Clauser, Zeilinger).

$|S| \leq 2$ (CHSH); quantum maximum $2\sqrt 2$

Bell statesch. 4

Four maximally entangled two-qubit states that form an orthonormal basis. They are used in quantum teleportation and quantum cryptography.

$\ket{\Phi^\pm} = \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}(\ket{00} \pm \ket{11})$, $\ket{\Psi^\pm} = \frac{1}{\sqrt 2}(\ket{01} \pm \ket{10})$

Bloch spherech. 1

Geometric picture of the state of a qubit: each pure state is a point on the sphere of radius 1. The north pole is $\ket{0}$, the south pole $\ket{1}$. Mixed states lie inside.

$\ket{\psi} = \cos\frac{\theta}{2}\ket{0} + e^{i\phi}\sin\frac{\theta}{2}\ket{1}$

Born rulech. 3

Probability of obtaining the eigenvalue $a_n$ (non-degenerate case) in a measurement on the state $\ket{\psi}$ (postulate 4).

$P(a_n) = |\braket{a_n|\psi}|^2$

Coherent statesch. 6

Eigenstates of the annihilation operator. They saturate the uncertainty relation and their mean values follow the classical motion. They give a good description of laser light.

$\hat a\ket{\alpha} = \alpha\ket{\alpha}$

Commutatorch. 2

$[\hat A, \hat B] = \hat A\hat B - \hat B\hat A$. Two observables that do not commute have, in general, no common eigenbasis: they cannot both be given well-defined values at the same time.

$[\hat x, \hat p] = i\hbar$

Creation and annihilation operatorsch. 6

Ladder operators of the oscillator: $\hat a$ lowers the state by one level, $\hat a^\dagger$ raises it by one level.

$\hat a = \sqrt{\frac{m\omega}{2\hbar}}\left(\hat x + \frac{i\hat p}{m\omega}\right)$

Decoherencech. 1

Loss of interference in a system through its interaction with the environment. It explains why superpositions of macroscopic objects are not observed.

Dirac notationch. 1

A ket $\ket{\psi}$ represents a state vector, a bra $\bra{\phi}$ the associated linear form. Their inner product is written $\braket{\phi|\psi}$.

$\braket{\phi|\psi} = \sum_i \phi_i^* \psi_i$

Entanglementch. 4

A state of several systems is entangled if it cannot be written as a product of states of each one. Measurements on the parts are then correlated beyond what a local description allows.

Fourier transformch. 5

Relates the position and momentum representations of the same state.

$\tilde\psi(p) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int \psi(x)\, e^{-ipx/\hbar}\,\mathrm{d}x$

Hamiltonianch. 3

The energy observable. It governs time evolution; its eigenstates are the stationary states.

$\hat H = \frac{\hat p^2}{2m} + V(\hat x)$

Hermite polynomialsch. 6

Polynomials that appear in the eigenfunctions of the harmonic oscillator, with $\xi = \sqrt{m\omega/\hbar}\,x$.

$\psi_n(x) = N_n H_n(\xi)\, e^{-\xi^2/2}$

Hermitian operatorch. 2

An operator equal to its adjoint, $\hat A = \hat A^\dagger$. Its eigenvalues are real; eigenvectors belonging to distinct eigenvalues are orthogonal.

$\braket{\phi|\hat A\psi} = \braket{\hat A\phi|\psi}$

Hilbert spacech. 1

A complete complex vector space with an inner product. The states of a system are normalised vectors of this space. For a qubit, it is $\mathbb{C}^2$.

Observablech. 2

A measurable physical quantity, represented by a Hermitian operator. Its eigenvalues are the possible results of a measurement.

Pauli matricesch. 2

Three 2×2 matrices that are both Hermitian and unitary. Together with the identity, they form a basis of the operators on a qubit. For a spin 1/2, $\hat S_i = \frac{\hbar}{2}\sigma_i$.

$\sigma_x = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, $\sigma_y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix}$, $\sigma_z = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}$

Postulates of quantum mechanicsch. 3

The six postulates of the course: (1) the state is a vector in a Hilbert space; (2) every measurable quantity is represented by a Hermitian operator; (3) the results of a measurement are its eigenvalues; (4) their probability follows the Born rule; (5) after the measurement, the state is projected onto the eigenspace obtained; (6) time evolution follows the Schrödinger equation.

Probability amplitudech. 1

A complex number associated with a possible outcome. The probability is the square of its modulus. When several indistinguishable paths lead to the same outcome, it is the amplitudes that add, not the probabilities.

$P(k) = |\braket{k|\psi}|^2$

Quantum harmonic oscillatorch. 6

A particle in a quadratic potential. Equally spaced energy levels, non-zero minimum energy. A model for molecular vibrations, phonons and the modes of the electromagnetic field.

$E_n = \hbar\omega\left(n + \frac{1}{2}\right)$, $n = 0, 1, 2, \dots$

Quantum teleportationch. 4

A protocol that transfers the state of a qubit to a distant qubit using an entangled pair and two classical bits. Neither matter nor information travels faster than light.

Qubitch. 1

A quantum system with two basis states $\ket{0}$ and $\ket{1}$. Its general state is a normalised superposition of these two states.

$\ket{\psi} = \alpha\ket{0} + \beta\ket{1}$, $|\alpha|^2 + |\beta|^2 = 1$

Schrödinger equationch. 3

Equation for the time evolution of the state of an isolated system (postulate 6). Its solutions of the form $e^{-iEt/\hbar}\ket{E}$ are the stationary states.

$i\hbar\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}\ket{\psi(t)} = \hat H\ket{\psi(t)}$

Spinch. 2

Intrinsic angular momentum, with no classical equivalent. For a spin 1/2, a measurement of $S_z$ gives only $\pm\hbar/2$ (Stern–Gerlach experiment).

$\hat S^2\ket{s, m_s} = \hbar^2 s(s+1)\ket{s, m_s}$

Superpositionch. 1

A linear combination of states. Before measurement, a system in a superposition has no definite value for the corresponding observable. This is not classical ignorance: the terms of the superposition can interfere.

$\ket{\psi} = \sum_i c_i \ket{\phi_i}$

Tensor productch. 4

Construction of the state space of a composite system from the spaces of its parts. For two qubits: $\mathbb{C}^2 \otimes \mathbb{C}^2 \simeq \mathbb{C}^4$.

$\ket{\psi} \otimes \ket{\phi} = \ket{\psi\phi}$

Uncertainty relationch. 2

For two observables, the product of the standard deviations is bounded by the mean value of their commutator. For position and momentum: $\Delta x\,\Delta p \geq \hbar/2$.

$\Delta A\,\Delta B \geq \frac{1}{2}|\langle[\hat A, \hat B]\rangle|$

Wave functionch. 5

Components of the state in the position basis. $|\psi(x)|^2$ is the probability density for detecting the particle at $x$.

$\psi(x, t) = \braket{x|\psi(t)}$

Wave packetch. 5

A superposition of plane waves localised in space. The narrower it is in position, the broader its momentum distribution.

$\psi(x, t) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int \tilde\psi(k)\, e^{i(kx - \omega t)}\,\mathrm{d}k$

Wave–particle dualitych. 1

A quantum object shows wave-like behaviour (interference) or particle-like behaviour (localised detection) depending on the set-up. Young’s slits lit one photon at a time show both: point-like impacts, and fringes as they accumulate.

$\lambda = h/p$ (de Broglie)
