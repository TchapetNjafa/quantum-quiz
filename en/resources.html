Going further

Resources

A short selection. Every link was checked in October 2026.

Start with the PHY321 Notebook: it follows the course section by section, with labs to try out.

Online courses

  • MIT OpenCourseWare, 8.04 Quantum Physics I ocw.mit.edu

    Full course by Allan Adams (2016): videos, notes and solved problems.

  • The Theoretical Minimum: Quantum Mechanics theoreticalminimum.com

    Recorded lectures by Leonard Susskind (Stanford), accessible with few prerequisites.

  • edX: quantum physics courses edx.org

    University courses, free in audit mode.

  • FUN MOOC fun-mooc.fr

    French online course platform; search for “physique quantique”. In French.

Simulations

  • QuVis (University of St Andrews) st-andrews.ac.uk

    Interactive simulations: single-photon experiments, spin, entanglement, potential wells.

  • PhET: quantum phenomena phet.colorado.edu

    Simulations from the University of Colorado, available in several languages (this link opens the French version).

  • Quirk algassert.com

    Quantum circuit simulator in the browser: gates, measurements, Bloch sphere for each qubit.

  • IBM Quantum Composer quantum.cloud.ibm.com

    Build a circuit, simulate it and, with a free account, run it on a real IBM Quantum Platform processor.

Computing tools

  • QuTiP qutip.org

    Free Python library for simulating quantum systems (spins, oscillators, dynamics).

  • Qiskit ibm.com

    Free Python library from IBM for writing and simulating quantum circuits.

  • IBM Quantum Learning quantum.cloud.ibm.com

    Free online courses on quantum information, using Qiskit.

Reading and discussion

  • Quanta Magazine: quantum physics quantamagazine.org

    Careful popular articles on current research.

  • arXiv, quant-ph arxiv.org

    Recent preprints in quantum physics (research level).

  • Physics Stack Exchange physics.stackexchange.com

    Moderated questions and answers, tag “quantum-mechanics”.
